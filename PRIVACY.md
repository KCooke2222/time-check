# Time Tracker – Privacy Policy

Time Tracker is a personal tool run by Kevin Cooke for his own use.

- It signs in with Google only to read the signed-in user's Google Calendar events and turn them into time reports.
- Calendar data is stored on Kevin's own computer and is never sold, shared or sent to third parties.
- Google user data is used only to provide this feature, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
- To delete your data or revoke access, remove Time Tracker at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or email thevoidkc@gmail.com.
